Privacy Policy

Your privacy and the confidentiality of your health data are of paramount importance to our medical practice.

Last Updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how the medical practice of Dr. Jithin Lal (Consultant Nephrologist at Amrutha Hospital, Kalamassery, Kochi) collects, utilizes, and protects information submitted by patients and visitors through this website.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our online appointment request or contact inquiry forms, you voluntarily submit personal information, which may include:

  • Full name
  • Mobile and WhatsApp contact number
  • Email address
  • Preferred date and time of clinical consultation
  • Reason for visit, brief medical symptoms, or report descriptions

2. How Your Information Is Used

The information you provide is utilized solely to:

  • Facilitate direct scheduling and coordination of your in-person consultation with the clinic desk at Amrutha Hospital.
  • Communicate appointment confirmation, clinic timing updates, or instructions regarding records to bring.
  • Answer specific administrative or clinical inquiries regarding nephrology services.

We do not sell, rent, commercialize, or share your contact or medical details with any third-party marketing companies.

3. WhatsApp Redirection & Communication

Our appointment and contact forms are designed for patient convenience by generating a structured text message that you review and send through the official WhatsApp application to our clinic desk (+91 97458 66333).

Messages sent via WhatsApp are subject to WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption and terms of service. By submitting a form, you consent to communicate with our clinic team via this channel.

4. Medical Confidentiality & Record Protection

In accordance with medical ethics established by the Kerala State Medical Councils (KSMC) and National Medical Commission guidelines, all clinical consultations, medical reports, diagnosis discussions, and treatment plans remain strictly confidential within the doctor-patient professional relationship.

5. Medical Disclaimer

The content published on this website—including articles in the Health Blog, answers in the FAQ, and description of medical conditions—is provided strictly for general public awareness and educational purposes. It does not constitute formal medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Submitting an inquiry through this website or initiating a message on WhatsApp does not establish a formal doctor-patient relationship until an in-person clinical consultation and medical examination are conducted.

6. Contact Regarding Privacy Inquiries

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to modify any information previously provided, please contact:
