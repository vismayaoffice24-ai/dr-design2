Clinical Practice

Specialized Nephrology Services

Comprehensive diagnostic, preventive, and therapeutic renal care delivered with precision, compassion, and evidence-based standards.

Areas of Care

Dedicated Kidney Medicine Services

Each consultation is backed by Dr. Jithin Lal's 15 years of clinical experience, ensuring individual treatment plans for every stage of renal disease.

Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) Management

Staging (Stages 1 through 5), evidence-based therapies to retard renal decline, blood pressure and proteinuria control, dietary mineral management, and cardiovascular protection.

  • eGFR & Albumin-Creatinine Ratio (ACR) tracking
  • Medication safety and nephrotoxicity avoidance
  • Anemia & bone-mineral metabolism stabilization

Dialysis Planning & Clinical Supervision

Holistic medical guidance for patients transitioning into renal replacement therapy. Overseeing hemodialysis and peritoneal dialysis prescriptions, adequacy, and vascular access health.

  • AV Fistula / Graft vascular access follow-up
  • Peritoneal dialysis training & infection prevention
  • Fluid status balance and electrolyte stabilization

Kidney Transplant Physician Follow-Up

Comprehensive medical care before and after kidney transplantation. Evaluation of prospective recipients and living donors, immunosuppressive drug monitoring, and long-term graft protection.

  • Pre-transplant fitness and immunologic screening
  • Post-transplant immunosuppression drug level tuning
  • Infection surveillance and graft longevity care

Renal Hypertension & Secondary High BP

Evaluation of resistant or early-onset hypertension linked to renal parenchymal disease or renal artery stenosis. Tailoring pharmacological treatments to shield cardiac and renal vasculature.

  • Investigation for renovascular hypertension
  • Combination therapy for resistant blood pressure
  • End-organ and ocular vascular protection

Glomerular Diseases & Nephrotic Syndrome

Thorough evaluation of persistent protein in urine (proteinuria), microscopic hematuria, body swelling (edema), and glomerulonephritis with referral for renal biopsy interpretation when warranted.

  • Edema (fluid retention) evaluation and relief
  • Immune-mediated glomerular disease management
  • Serum albumin and lipid profile management

Preventive Renal Health Checkups

Proactive kidney screenings for individuals living with diabetes, long-standing hypertension, recurring kidney stones, or a family history of kidney failure.

  • Early microalbuminuria detection tests
  • Serum creatinine, urea, and electrolyte analysis
  • Personalized diet, hydration, and salt advice
Consultation Guide

What to Expect During Your Visit

To ensure your consultation at Amrutha Hospital is productive, please review the simple recommendations below.

1. Bring All Previous Medical Records

Bring previous blood tests (creatinine, urea, HbA1c, hemoglobin), urine routine and microalbumin tests, ultrasound abdomen scans, and discharge summaries from prior hospital stays. Comparing historical trends is essential in nephrology.

2. Bring Your Current Medication List

Bring the exact prescription strips or bottles of all current tablets, including blood pressure medications, diabetes pills/insulin, water pills (diuretics), and any herbal or over-the-counter supplements you may be taking.

3. Clear, Written Action Plan

Following examination and record review, Dr. Jithin Lal will outline diagnosis, stage of kidney function, recommended dosage adjustments, dietary goals, and a specific follow-up date.
